## The brand

**Northwind Skin Co.** — DTC skincare. Founded 2023. Four SKUs: a cleanser, a
niacinamide serum, a barrier cream, an SPF. $34–52. DTC plus 180 Ulta doors since
March. 61,400 followers on Instagram.

Positioning as they state it: *clinical formulation, honestly labelled.* Every
product page leads with the active and its concentration.

## What Atlas has read

340 posts, 289 analyzed (85% of 340). Window: 14 months, oldest 2025-06-11.
Posting ~4.2×/week.

**Their own content themes** *(casing-normalized)*

| Theme | Posts |
|---|---|
| Clinical | 89 |
| Minimal | 76 |
| Ingredient | 64 |
| Product-only | 71 |
| Bright | 41 |
| Person on camera | 22 |

**Engagement** — median 412 likes / 31 comments / 96 saves.

**Top three by saves**

| Post | Saves | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| "My actual 6am, unedited" | 12,400 | A routine reel. One take, no captions burnt in |
| Dermatologist Q&A | 4,900 | Person on camera answering audience questions |
| "Why we changed the cap" | 3,100 | Founder to camera, no product claim |

**The pattern in the numbers:** posts with a person on camera average **4.1×** the
saves of product-only posts. Only 22 of 340 posts have one.

Four of 118 creator posts mention formulation at all.

## The gap

> **You market chemistry. Your creators and your audience both talk about routine.
> You're the only one in the conversation naming the ingredient.**

Evidence: 64 of your own posts lead on ingredient; 4 of 118 creator posts mention
it; your audience saves routine content 4.1× more than product-only.

## Supporting insights

1. **A person on camera is worth 4.1× in saves** — and you've done it 22 times in
   340 posts. → *decision: change what the brief asks for.*
2. **Your best post has no product claim in it.** "Why we changed the cap" is a
   founder talking about packaging, at 7.5× your median saves. → *decision: test
   whether trust content outperforms proof content.*
3. **Your 4 ingredient-led creator posts are your 4 weakest creator posts**, each
   below that creator's own median. → *decision: stop briefing formulation.*
4. **Nobody owns "morning" in your category.** 52 creator posts sit in it, none of
   your competitors' branded hashtags do. → *decision: a territory worth claiming.*
5. **Since the Ulta launch your comment volume is up 71%** while likes are flat —
   people are asking questions, not admiring. → *decision: answer them on camera.*
